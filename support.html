Help for crew, clients and the office.

Need a hand now?
Email andrew@painternetwork.com.au or call +61 422 615 139.
We reply on working days, usually within a few hours.

I can't sign in

Accounts are set up by the Prime office. Use the same mobile number or email the office has on file. We text or email you a 6-digit code. If the code doesn't arrive, check the number, wait a minute, then tap Send again. If it still doesn't work, contact us and we will check your account.

I'm not getting notifications

On your iPhone, open Settings, then Notifications, then Prime Painting, and turn on Allow Notifications.

A photo won't upload

Check you have signal. In Settings, then Prime Painting, make sure Camera and Photos are allowed. Try again after closing and reopening the app.

I'm out of paint or something is wrong on site

Use the Notify office button on the job screen. For anything unsafe, stop work and call the office.

Delete my account

In the app, open your account and tap Delete my account. Or email us and we will do it for you. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.

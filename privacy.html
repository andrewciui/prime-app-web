Privacy Policy

Prime Painting QLD app. Last updated: 5 November 2026.

This app is run by Painter Network Group Pty Ltd, trading as Prime Painting QLD ("Prime", "we", "us"), 322 Wecker Rd, Carindale QLD 4152, Australia. We handle your personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Who the app is for

The app is used by our painters and subcontractors (crew), our office team, and the clients we are painting for. We set up each account. You can't sign up on your own.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number and email address, and your role (crew, office or client).
  • Job information: the sites you are working on or having painted, daily briefs, tasks, colour choices, variations, payments and progress updates.
  • Content you add: chat messages, site notes, reports, and photos you take or choose from your library.
  • Work records: pre-start and safety sign-offs, hours, and the time of confirmations (for example, when a brief was read).
  • Device information: a push notification token if you turn notifications on, and basic technical logs to fix faults.

We do not collect your location in the background. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your information.

How we use it

  • To run jobs: tell crew where to be and what to do, record safety sign-offs, and keep clients updated.
  • To sign you in with a one-time code, Google or Apple.
  • To send notifications you ask for, such as tomorrow's brief or a message from the office.
  • To keep records we need for safety, insurance, payroll and disputes.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

Who can see it

Crew see the jobs they are rostered on. Clients see only their own project. The Prime office team can see everything. Photos and messages in a job chat are visible to the people on that job.

We use trusted service providers to run the app, such as cloud hosting and database, sign-in, SMS and email delivery, and push notifications. They handle your information only to provide those services to us. Some of these providers store data outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect health and safety.

How long we keep it

We keep job records for as long as we need them for safety, insurance, tax and warranty reasons, usually up to 7 years. Chat messages and photos are kept with the job record. When you delete your account, we remove your login and personal profile, and keep only job records we are required to keep.

Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app: Account, then Delete my account. You can also email andrew@painternetwork.com.au and we will do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and to correct it. Email us at the address below. If you are not happy with how we handled a privacy issue, you can complain to us first. If we don't resolve it, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Security

We use encrypted connections and sign-in controls so people only see what their role allows. No system is perfect. If we learn of a breach likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the regulator as the law requires.

Children

The app is not for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
